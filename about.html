OUR STORY

ROOTED IN PONDICHERRY. INSPIRED BY YOUR STORY.

Founded in 2020, Le Photogram is a wedding photography and film studio based in Puducherry. We document celebrations across South India, with a focus on the people, traditions and emotions that make every wedding personal.

For us, the story lives in more than the ceremony. It is in the excitement before it begins, your parents’ expressions, your friends’ laughter and the quiet moments you share.

We take time to understand what matters to you, offering gentle guidance when needed and space for moments to unfold naturally. Through photographs and films, we create memories that bring you back to how your wedding felt.

“The team took every emotion of our wedding and turned it into films and photographs that bring tears of joy every single time.”

Every ritual was captured with such quiet respect and genuine affection. What impressed us most was how comfortable they made our parents and entire family feel throughout all four days.
