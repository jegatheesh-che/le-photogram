FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Clear, transparent answers regarding our approach, booking process, South Indian wedding ceremonies, deliverables, and travel across Tamil Nadu & India.

We recommend getting in touch as soon as your wedding date is confirmed. Popular dates and auspicious muhurtham days can book up early. Share your date, venue and planned celebrations with us, and we'll check our availability.
A 10% booking advance of the total package amount is required to confirm your event date.

• 10% booking advance: Payable when confirming your booking.
• 75% event payment: Payable one day before or on the event day, once the event is confirmed to proceed. This brings the total paid to 85%.
• 15% final payment: Payable after delivery of the agreed final photographs and films.
We understand that plans may change. Our refund policy is based on the amount paid at the time of cancellation:

• After paying the initial 10% advance: We refund 5% of the total package value and retain the remaining 5%, regardless of the cancellation reason.
• After paying 85%, for a genuine emergency or unavoidable circumstance: We refund the full 85% already paid.
• After paying 85%, for other cancellation reasons: We refund 50% of the amount paid (equivalent to 42.5% of the total package value).

If you need to reschedule, please contact us as early as possible. Alternative dates are subject to availability and mutual agreement.
