FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Clear, transparent answers regarding our artistic approach, booking protocols, travel coordination, and archival heirloom deliverables.

Most couples book our studio between 9 and 18 months in advance, particularly for peak European summer weekends (June through September) and autumn in New York. We accept a strictly capped limit of 22 weddings annually to ensure personal devotion and rapid delivery for every couple.
A signed digital commission agreement and a 30% initial retainer secure your date exclusively. The remaining balance is split into two equal milestones: 35% midway through your planning process, and the final 35% thirty days prior to your celebration date.
If you need to reschedule, we will gladly transfer 100% of your retainer to any mutually available date within 12 months of your original date at no penalty fee. In the event of unforeseen cancellations, our agreement outlines clear, balanced protocols.
Our philosophy is predominantly documentary and unobtrusive. We believe the most timeless photographs and films emerge when you are completely immersed in your celebration rather than posing for a camera. During couple portraits, we provide gentle, intuitive guidance regarding light and motion so you always look effortless and never feel stiff.
Yes. We embrace a hybrid methodology, pairing state-of-the-art medium-format digital sensors for speed and low-light precision with authentic 35mm film cameras and vintage 1970s Super 8mm cameras for rich, tactile, and nostalgic celluloid grain.
We personally curate and license the musical score from prestigious independent music libraries, selecting compositions that elevate the emotional arc and rhythm of your celebration. We always welcome your personal Spotify playlists to understand your acoustic tastes and inspirations.
For a full 10-hour wedding, you will receive between 750 and 950 curated, film-graded images. An editorial sneak peek (40–50 photos) is delivered within 7 business days following your celebration. Your full online vault and 4K film master are completed within 8 to 10 weeks.
We do not deliver unedited RAW files. Color grading, exposure calibration, and careful artistic curation are inseparable parts of the Le PhotoGRAM aesthetic. Every image delivered in your private vault is at full resolution, print-ready, and completely free of watermarks.
We begin by curating a complete digital layout draft that tells the emotional story of your day. You then have full collaborative access through our proofing interface to review spreads, swap images, and choose your cover cloth (Italian natural linen or velvet) and foil embossing.
Yes! Over 60% of our annual commissions take place outside the United States. We have documented celebrations across France, Italy, Greece, Japan, the UK, Mexico, and beyond. All travel logistics (flights, lodging, transfers) are handled seamlessly by our studio and quoted as a flat, transparent line item.
Every photographer and filmmaker shoots on dual memory card slots, meaning two identical copies of every photo and video frame are saved instantly at the moment of capture. We also travel with redundant camera bodies, backup lenses, independent audio recorders, and external drives.
Yes, Le PhotoGRAM carries a $2,000,000 worldwide commercial general liability and equipment insurance policy. We gladly provide customized Certificates of Insurance (COI) directly to your venue or wedding planner upon request.
