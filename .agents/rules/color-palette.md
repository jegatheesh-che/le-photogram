# Le PhotoGRAM - Bright Theme & Color Palette Rules

Enforce the official Le PhotoGRAM **Bright Theme** tokens and rules across all pages:

## Bright Theme Rules:
- Primary Canvas: Pure White `#FFFFFF` & Soft Porcelain `#FAF8F5`
- Primary Typography: Cosmos Blue `#003049` (rich high-contrast dark ink)
- Warm Luxury Contrast: Varden `#FDF0D5` for testimonial cards, footer, pill badges, and warm moments
- High-Impact Accents: Crimson Blaze `#C1121F` for primary buttons, active tabs, section numbers `(01)`, hover rings
- Deep Regal Accents: Gochujang Red `#780000` for hover depth, author names, and avatar rings
- Secondary Details: Blue Marble `#3A6885` / `#669BBC` for section eyebrows, dates, metadata, and subtle borders
