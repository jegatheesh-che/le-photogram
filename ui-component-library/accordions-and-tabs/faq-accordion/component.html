Most couples book our studio between 9 and 18 months in advance, particularly for peak European summer weekends (June through September) and autumn in New York. We accept a strictly capped limit of 22 weddings annually to ensure personal devotion and rapid delivery for every couple.
A signed digital commission agreement and a 30% initial retainer secure your date exclusively. The remaining balance is split into two equal milestones: 35% midway through your planning process, and the final 35% thirty days prior to your celebration date.
Our philosophy is predominantly documentary and unobtrusive. We believe the most timeless photographs and films emerge when you are completely immersed in your celebration rather than posing for a camera. During couple portraits, we provide gentle, intuitive guidance regarding light and motion so you always look effortless and never feel stiff.
You will receive an editorial sneak peek of 50 to 75 finished film-graded images within 7 days of your wedding. The complete digital vault is delivered within 8 to 10 weeks, followed by album design consultation and hand-binding in Italy.
